Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

At Creo, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy describes how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use our SaaS creator studio platform.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you directly provide to us, including your email address, first name, last name, and preferences when you register for an account. If you sign up using Google, we retrieve your Google identification token, email address, name, and profile photo avatar.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your information to operate and manage your account, track and enforce generation limits, run competitor intelligence audits, send system alerts, and provide support services. We do not sell or monetize your individual data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement secure cryptographic password hashing (bcrypt), cookie protection schemes (httpOnly), and token validation layers to prevent unauthorized access. However, no transmission over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute protection.

4. Your Rights

You have full control over your profile details. You can update your name, toggle notifications, or request permanent deletion of your account and all associated scripts or channel reports from MongoDB at any time via the User Profile view inside the Studio.

5. Cookies

We use cookies to maintain your logged-in session. Toggling or blocking session cookies will prevent the application from verifying your credentials and loading the protected study tools.
