Refund Policy

Last updated: June 4, 2026

We want you to be completely satisfied with our SaaS creator studio. This refund policy governs any request for refunds or billing adjustments on our paid tiers.

1. 14-Day Satisfaction Guarantee

If you are not satisfied with your Pro or Creator subscription plan, you are eligible to request a full refund within 14 days of your initial purchase date. Refund requests made after the 14-day window are generally not accepted.

2. Usage Limits Qualification

To prevent abuse of our refund policy, you qualify for a satisfaction refund only if you have consumed fewer than 50 script generations or hook test actions during the active 14-day refund request period.

3. Recurring Renewal Charges

Subscriptions renew automatically on a monthly cycle. You must cancel your plan before the renewal date to avoid future billing. We do not provide refunds or prorated credits for partial-month usage after a subscription has renewed.

4. Submitting a Request

To request a refund under the satisfaction guarantee, please submit a message using our active contact form on the Contact Us page, or reach out to our support email with your registration details. We aim to process approved refund claims within 5-7 business days.
